# Superannuation Domain Skills

Skills that help AI correctly interpret and document superannuation concepts, processes, and terminology.

## Example Skills
- Explain Australian superannuation concepts (contributions, preservation, insurance, advice, etc.) in the context of the fund’s platforms
- Map business processes to the platforms and systems involved
- Identify data entities (member, account, product, contribution, cash receipt, etc.) and their relationships
- Document timing and sequencing constraints common in superannuation processes (e.g. contribution processing, insurance underwriting, advice generation)
- Flag regulatory or process considerations relevant to test data and scenarios

These skills provide the domain grounding needed when documenting platforms or when generating/healing automation that exercises superannuation flows.
